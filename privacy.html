Privacy policy

How Blockwork handles information — yours, and your clients'.

The short version

Blockwork has no account, no server and no analytics. Everything you and your clients put into it stays in the app's own storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded, because there is nowhere to upload it to.

One thing worth being clear about: Blockwork holds information about other people — your clients. We never receive it, but you are the one responsible for it. See "Your clients' information" below.

What Blockwork stores, and where

Everything is written to a database inside Blockwork's own container on your device:

  • Client records: name, email, phone, goal, injury notes, general notes and start date
  • Programmes: weeks, days, exercises, sets, reps, loads, tempo, rest and notes
  • Logged sessions: dates, weights, reps, RPE, session notes and duration
  • Your exercise library, including anything you add yourself
  • Your own coach and business name, used on the plans you export
  • Your preferences: units and default rest

That database is covered by your device's own encryption whenever the device is locked, and it is included in an encrypted iPhone or iCloud device backup if you make one. Blockwork itself never uploads it.

Your clients' information

Blockwork is a professional tool, so most of what it holds describes somebody other than you — including injury notes, which in many places count as health information about an identifiable person.

We never see any of it. It is not transmitted to us, and there is no copy anywhere but your device. In data-protection terms that makes you the controller of your clients' information and Blockwork simply the notebook you keep it in. Practically, that means:

  • Tell your clients what you record about them, as you would with any notes
  • Only record what you actually need to coach them safely
  • If a client asks for their record or asks you to delete it, you can do both inside the app — export their training log as CSV, or delete the client, which removes their programmes and every logged session with them
  • Keep your device locked with a passcode or Face ID; it is the only thing standing between their information and whoever picks the device up

We cannot answer a request about your client's data, because we have never held it.

Subscriptions

The Coach Pass is sold and processed by Apple. When you subscribe, restore a purchase or manage a subscription, you are dealing with Apple and with the payment details already on your Apple Account. Blockwork never sees your name, your email address, your card or any other identifier — only whether this device currently holds a valid subscription. Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Exports and sharing

When you export a programme as a PDF or a training log as CSV, the file is written to a temporary folder on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is entirely your choice — Blockwork has no part in it, and no copy is kept or sent anywhere. Files you send by email or message leave your device under the terms of whatever app you send them with.

What Blockwork does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or profile of any kind
  • No advertising, ad networks, or advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage measurement
  • No tracking, and nothing shared with other apps or companies
  • No third-party software development kits at all
  • No cloud sync, and no backup held by us

Deleting information

  • Delete a client removes that client, their programmes and every session logged against them, immediately and completely.
  • Deleting the app removes the whole database with it.

There is nothing to request from us and nobody to ask, because we never had a copy.

Children

Blockwork is a tool for working fitness professionals. It is not directed at children and collects nothing from anybody, including children. If you coach under-18s, the records you keep about them are yours to handle under the rules that apply where you practise.

Not a medical record

Injury notes in Blockwork are coaching notes, not clinical records. The app does not assess anybody, does not screen for injury or illness, and is not a medical device. It is not built to meet HIPAA, and it should not be used as a system of record for regulated clinical information.
