# blockwork-support

Support page, privacy policy and terms of use for **Blockwork**, a training
programme builder for personal trainers on iOS.

Published with GitHub Pages from the `main` branch:

| Page | URL | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Support | https://williamscott701.github.io/blockwork-support/ | App Store Connect **Support URL** (required) |
| Privacy | https://williamscott701.github.io/blockwork-support/privacy.html | App Store Connect **Privacy Policy URL** (required) |
| Terms | https://williamscott701.github.io/blockwork-support/terms.html | Subscription EULA, linked from the in-app paywall (required for auto-renewables) |

`.nojekyll` stops GitHub Pages running these through Jekyll.

The terms are a custom EULA rather than Apple's standard one, so they carry the
minimum terms Apple requires: acknowledgement that the agreement is not with
Apple, scope of licence, maintenance, warranty, product claims, intellectual
property, legal compliance, third-party terms, and Apple as third-party
beneficiary.
