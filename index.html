Common questions

Does my client need the app?

No. Blockwork is built for the coach. You hand the programme over as a PDF — by message, email, print, whatever you already use. Nothing asks your client to install or sign up for anything.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone or iPad, and nowhere else. Blockwork has no account, no server and no sync. That also means it is not backed up anywhere by us — an encrypted iPhone or iCloud device backup is what protects you against a lost phone.

What does the free version do?

One client and one programme, in full: build it, generate the block, log sessions. Pro removes the limits and adds the client PDF, progress analytics, templates and CSV export.

How do I cancel?

In the Settings app under your Apple Account, or through the App Store. Cancelling stops the renewal; you keep Pro until the period you already paid for ends. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Pro exports any programme and the full training log for any client as CSV, and any programme as a PDF. There is no lock-in and no export fee.
